Kilotest exemplars

This page introduces a collection of minimal pages that exemplify types of front-end defects. Each page in the collection is self-documenting: it describes its purpose, contains the exemplary defects, and provides defect-free counterparts for contrast. The pages exist so that the results of testing them can be compared with their intended contents, revealing whether each rule engine of Kilotest behaves as expected.

The pages of this collection are registered as the “Kilotest exemplars” site. The listPages tool of Kilotest, applied to that site, lists the exemplar pages that have been tested, and each listed page links to the issues reported in its latest test.
