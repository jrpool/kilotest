Accessibility statement

This statement applies to the Kilotest web site at kilotest.com. It was last updated in October 2026.

Policy

Kilotest intends to design and implement a simple, intuitive, and accessible web interface that conforms to Level AAA of Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 and best practices defined by various accessibility experts. One requirement that Kilotest imposes on itself is that no page exhibits any issues other than lowest-priority issues when tested by Kilotest itself. Any lowest-priority issues are remediated or their justification is documented on this page.

Known limitations

Reports about third-party pages quote elements of those pages, such as tag names, attributes, and text excerpts. Kilotest does not control the accessibility of quoted content or of the tested pages themselves.

Comments

If you encounter an accessibility barrier on this site, or if any content is hard for you to perceive or operate, email info@kilotest.com or submit an issue on GitHub. Reported barriers are treated as defects and prioritized for remediation.
