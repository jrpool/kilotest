What Kilotest does

Kilotest tests publicly reachable web pages for front-end quality (accessibility, usability, and web standards) with an ensemble of rule engines, stores the results as reports, and makes the reports publicly available through its web site, API, and MCP server. Owners of tested pages can request deletion of reports under the privacy policy.

What you submit

When you request or order a test or retest, you ask Kilotest to fetch the page and publish a report about it, including findings about its defects and exerpts from it. You do not need the page owner’s permission to do so. Submit only publicly reachable URLs. Do not submit URLs that require credentials, contain secrets, or could cause secret or unlawful content to appear in a Kilotest report. Kilotest may reject any request or order.
