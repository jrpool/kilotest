Kilotest: Connect an AI Platform

Purpose

This tutorial shows you how to make Kilotest available to AI assistants. Suppose:

  • You use an AI platform, like Claude, Mistral Vibe, or Perplexity Pro.
  • You want to audit the quality of a web page.

Then the models on your AI platform can use Kilotest if you make Kilotest available on the platform.

What AI platform do you use?

  • Claude.ai in a browser or Claude Desktop
  • Mistral Vibe
  • Perplexity Pro in a browser

Claude.ai in a browser or Claude Desktop

  1. Start a new task.
  2. Expand the left sidebar to see the labels.
  3. Click the Customize button.
  4. Click the Connectors button.
  5. In the Add list, click the Add custom connector button.
  6. Enter Kilotest into the Name input.
  7. Enter https://kilotest.com/mcp into the Remote MCP server URL input.
  8. Leave the OAuth inputs empty.
  9. Click the Add button.
  10. For maximum autonomy, click Always allow for each tool.
  11. Click the x button or press the Escape key to close the dialog.

Mistral Vibe

  1. Choose the Work tab.
  2. Click Connect your apps for better answers.
  3. Click the + Add connector button.
  4. Click the Add a custom connector button.
  5. Enter Kilotest into the Connector name input.
  6. Enter https://kilotest.com/mcp into the Server input.
  7. Click the Add connector button.
  8. Toggle the Ask/Allow buttons to show Allow.

Perplexity Pro in a browser

  1. After signing in, click the Customize button in the left sidebar.
  2. Click the + Custom connector menu button.
  3. Click the Add MCP connector menu item.
  4. Enter Kilotest into the Name input.
  5. Enter https://kilotest.com/mcp into the MCP server URL input.
  6. Click the Advanced menu button to open the advanced settings.
  7. Under Authentication, change the authentication type to None.
  8. Check the I understand … checkbox.
  9. Click the Add button.

Once you add Kilotest, it may become available in the Perplexity app on your computer or mobile device, too.

Other AI platforms

You can connect Kilotest to some other AI platforms, too. The instructions above are limited to platforms that are either free or relatively inexpensive (US$20 per month or less). If you discover an AI platform that should be added to this page, please submit a comment below.
