Kilotest: Connect an AI Platform

Purpose

This tutorial shows you how to make Kilotest available to AI assistants. Suppose:

  • You use an AI platform, like Claude, ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Mistral Vibe.
  • You want to audit the quality of a web page.

Then the models on your AI platform can use Kilotest if you make Kilotest available on the platform.

What AI platform do you use?

  • Claude.ai in a browser or Claude Desktop
  • ChatGPT
  • Perplexity
  • Mistral Vibe

Claude.ai in a browser or Claude Desktop

To make Kilotest available on either Claude platform:

  1. Start a new task.
  2. Expand the left sidebar to see the labels.
  3. Click Customize.
  4. Click Connectors.
  5. In the Add list, click Add custom connector.
  6. Enter Kilotest into the Name input.
  7. Enter https://kilotest.com/mcp into the Remote MCP server URL input.
  8. Leave the OAuth inputs empty.
  9. Click Add.
  10. For maximum autonomy, click Always allow for each tool.
  11. Click the x button or press the Escape key to close the dialog.

Mistral Vibe

To make Kilotest available on Mistral Vibe:

  1. Choose the Work tab.
  2. Click Connect your apps for better answers
  3. Click the + Add connector button.
  4. Click the Add a custom connector button.
  5. Enter Kilotest into the Connector name input.
  6. Enter https://kilotest.com/mcp into the Server input.
  7. Click the Add connector button.
  8. Toggle the Ask/Allow buttons to show Allow.
