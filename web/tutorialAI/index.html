Kilotest and AI

Introduction

Kilotest is designed for use by artificial intelligence. When you ask AI assistants to help you diagnose and improve web quality, they can use Kilotest for the most complete and accurate results. This guide tells you how to make Kilotest available on AI platforms.

Step-by-step instructions

ChatGPT

These instructions apply to ChatGPTPlus and Pro plans used in a web browser.

  1. Click Plugins in the sidebar.
  2. In the Add list, select Add custom MCP server.
  3. Enter Kilotest as the Name.
  4. Enter Test web-page quality as the Description.
  5. Enter https://kilotest.com/mcp as the Server URL Connection.
  6. Set the Authentication to No authentication.
  7. Check I understand and want to continue.
  8. Click Create as a plugin.
  9. Wait for the plugin to load.
  10. Click Connect Kilotest.
  11. Kilotest is now connected will appear and disappear.

ChatGPT can then use Kilotest to answer your questions. To say you want it used, you can enter @ Kilotest as the start of any message.

Claude

These instructions apply to Claude.ai in a browser and Claude Desktop.

  1. Start a new task.
  2. Expand the left sidebar to see the labels.
  3. Click the Customize button.
  4. Click the Connectors button.
  5. Enter Kilotest in the Search input.
  6. Select Kilotest from the search results.
  7. Click the Connect to Claude button.
  8. If you wish, click the Your tool permissions button and adjust the settings for the Kilotest tools.
Gemini

These instructions apply to Google Gemini in a browser, if you are at least 18 years old, are in the United States, are signed in to a personal (not a work or school) Google Account with the Keep Activity setting on, and are using English. Google is rolling out custom apps gradually; on some plans the Custom apps section requires a Google AI Pro or Ultra subscription.

  1. Click the Settings button at the bottom left.
  2. Click Connected Apps. If you do not see it, click Personal Intelligence first.
  3. Under Custom apps, click Add a custom app.
  4. Enter https://kilotest.com/mcp as the MCP server URL.
  5. Click Next.
  6. Follow the on-screen instructions and approve the connection.

Once you add Kilotest, it also becomes available in the Gemini app on your mobile device. To make Gemini use Kilotest for a prompt, enter @ and select Kilotest.

Grok

These instructions apply to Grok on any plan, including the free plan.

  1. Open the Connectors page.
  2. Click the New Connector button.
  3. Click Custom.
  4. Enter Kilotest as the name.
  5. Enter https://kilotest.com/mcp as the server address.
  6. Save the connector.
Manus

These instructions apply to Manus.

  1. Open Connectors.
  2. Click Create.
  3. Click Add MCP by URL.
  4. Enter Kilotest into the Server name input.
  5. Enter https://kilotest.com/mcp into the Server URL input.
  6. Save the connector.
Perplexity

These instructions apply to Perplexity Pro in a browser.

  1. After signing in, open the Plugins menu.
  2. Click Add custom, then Connector.
  3. Click Add MCP connector, then Remote.
  4. Enter Kilotest into the Name input.
  5. Enter https://kilotest.com/mcp into the MCP server URL input.
  6. Under Authentication, change the authentication type to None.
  7. Check the I understand … checkbox.
  8. Click the Add button.

Once you add Kilotest, it may become available in the Perplexity app on your computer or mobile device, too.

Vibe

These instructions apply to Mistral Vibe.

  1. Choose the Work tab.
  2. Click Connect your apps for better answers.
  3. Click the + Add connector button.
  4. Click the Add a custom connector button.
  5. Enter Kilotest into the Connector name input.
  6. Enter https://kilotest.com/mcp into the Server input.
  7. Click the Add connector button.
  8. Toggle the Ask/Allow buttons to show Allow.

Other AI platforms

You can connect Kilotest to some other AI platforms, too. The instructions above are limited to consumer platforms that are either free or relatively inexpensive (US$20 per month or less). Business platforms such as ChatGPT Business, and developer platforms such as Devin Desktop, also permit you to enable Kilotest.

Feel free to submit a comment below about empowering any AI platform to use Kilotest.

You can also submit an issue on the Kilotest GitHub repository.
