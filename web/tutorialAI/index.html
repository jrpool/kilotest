Connect an AI Platform to Kilotest

Purpose

This tutorial shows you how to make Kilotest available to AI assistants. Suppose:

  • You use an AI platform, like Claude.ai, Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Perplexity, Cursor, Gemini, or ChatGPT.
  • You want to audit the quality of a web page.

Then the models on your AI platform can use Kilotest if you make Kilotest available on the platform.

Claude.ai

Claude.ai is an AI platform that you can use for free in any web browser. To make Kilotest available on Claude.ai:

  • Start a new task.
  • Expand the left sidebar to see the labels.
  • Click Customize.
  • Click Connectors.
  • In the Add list, click Add custom connector.
  • Enter Kilotest into the Name input.
  • Enter https://kilotest.com/mcp into the Remote MCP server URL input.
  • Leave the OAuth inputs empty.
  • Click Add.
  • Decide whether Claude.ai needs your approval to use each Kilotesw tool.
  • Clicking the x button or by press the Escape key to close the dialog.
